DmITE

Directivity of Musical Instruments in Teaching Environments: listen to an instrument recorded from different directions while the IEEE 1599 score follows the performance.

  • Choose an instrument above each video and drag the red marker around the dial, or focus the dial and use the arrow keys, to pick a recording angle.
  • The speaker button chooses which panel you hear. Click the score to jump to a note.
  • Tempo changes from 50% to 100% in 5% steps; the centre button restores 100%. One note plays a single note at a time, Melody plays continuously.
  • Selected assets stay in this browser. MP4/QuickTime video uses optional Mediabunny container reading and native WebCodecs decoding; unsupported codecs remain unavailable.
